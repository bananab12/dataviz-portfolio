**Question.** The final figure "27 goals" hides the interesting part — when those
goals actually arrived. Who led in autumn, winter, who closed the gap in late spring, and who
kept a steady pace across all 38 matches?

![Running total of goals for the five leading scorers](specs/pl-race.vl.json)

**Decision.** Cumulative lines per matchweek: the running total makes the plateaus and the steps visible.
Five best scoring players is the limit before the lines turn into a non-informative plot; each one is labelled at its end, 
so colour is not the only way to distinguish players.

**What it shows.** Haaland breaks clear after matchweek 10 and never gives the lead
back. Igor Thiago, by contrast, has two visible plateaus (matchweeks 12–18 and 24–28) and still finishes
second with 22. The most interesting part is at the bottom: Watkins, João Pedro and
Gibbs-White run almost the whole season together and only separate in the last rounds.
